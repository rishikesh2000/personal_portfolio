We built the three programs inside KT AI Station, a WSIS Prize 2026 Champion

What is the WSIS Prize?: The information-society award run by the ITU, the UN’s agency for digital technology. In 2026 it named KT AI Station, KT’s mobile AI classroom for schools in remote regions, a Champion.

  • Run by: ITU · UN
  • Category: Access to information
  • Named: 2026 Champion

  • Deepfake

    KT AI Station · KT · 2025 — 2026

    Visitors photograph their face at the kiosk and pick a scene card. About 30 seconds later a film plays with that face swapped in, followed by how deepfake harm spreads and how to prevent it.

  • Fake news

    KT AI Station · KT · 2025 — 2026

    Two news reports play, and visitors pick the one made with AI or answer that both are fake. An answer film follows, then a comparison with what others chose for the same pair.

  • Digital Citizen Type Test (DCTI)

    KT AI Station · KT · 2025 — 2026

    Twenty-seven picture items assess how visitors think, learn and act online and sort them into one of eight digital citizen types. Results come as a type card, a matching job and per-domain scores.

  • Jimssalabium

    KT × ENA · KT Gwanghwamun building, Seoul · August 2026

    A popup kiosk for Jimssalabium, an ENA travel show. It reads the barcodes on two packing cards, matches a destination and prints the result on a receipt.

  • Me in a Genre

    Millie’s Library × KT Onmaji · KT Onmaji flagship store, Gwanghwamun, Seoul · 2026

    A Millie’s Library kiosk that finds the genre that suits a visitor from a face photo and a reading-style test, then makes a book cover with the visitor as its hero.

  • Today’s Picnic

    KT Onmaji · KT Onmaji flagship store, Gwanghwamun, Seoul · 2026

    A kiosk whose travel-style test finds a taste for travel visitors didn’t know they had. It shows the recommended place in a film, then a travel guide and places to eat.

  • I Am Solo in Gwanghwamun

    KT Onmaji × ENA · Pop-up at the KT Onmaji flagship store, Gwanghwamun, Seoul · 2025

    A portrait kiosk for the pop-up of ENA’s I Am Solo. Visitors have their face photographed, get an AI character profile and a talent-show result, and take them home by QR code and as a print.

  • On-site booking

    In-house · KT AI Station venues · 2026

    Visitors book a session from a printed QR code and get a text five minutes before it starts. Staff follow each session’s bookings live on the laptop on site.

  • WIS 2026 Interactive Player

    KT · KT booth, World IT Show 2026, COEX, Seoul · April 2026

    A full-screen video player driven by a Taiko no Tatsujin drum controller. A hit on the left or right side plays that side’s film once, then the loop resumes.

From idea to something that works.

We check every step for ourselves, and finish one experience at a time.

  1. Concept: One core.

    First we decide the one thing people will remember.

  2. Prototype: An idea that works.

    Not with words or pictures, but with a draft that actually runs. Decisions come sooner, and there is less trial and error.

  3. Test: A finish that has been tested.

    We try it on the devices and in the space it is built for, so problems turn up in the studio, not on site.
